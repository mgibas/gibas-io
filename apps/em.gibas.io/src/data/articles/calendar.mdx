Now that you know what's your job is, let's see how you can organize your time to make sure you're doing it well.
Everyone is different and you'll need to find your own rhythm and structure but your calendar can be divided into couple different slots:

- One on ones <TheJobBadge job="growth" /> <TheJobBadge job="result" />
- Team meetings <TheJobBadge job="team" /> <TheJobBadge job="result" />
- Project meetings <TheJobBadge job="result" />
- Your contributions <TheJobBadge job="result" />

<CalendarEntryHeading copy="One on ones" class="rotate-2 max-w-80" startTime="10:00" endTime="11:00" theme="yellow" />

- **Once a week** or every other week - you can adjust based of how well you know your people, how experienced they are
  etc. You can also have different schedule for different people if necessary.
- **30-60min** - It's plenty. My baseline is 30 minutes and sometimes it's a quick, 15 minutes, check-in and sometimes it will go over 1 hour.
- **Don't schedule it back to back** - it's one of those meetings that you want to have some margin (15-30min)
- **Best before lunch** (before afternoon crash) but not too early - 10-11 is your best shot.

<CalendarEntryHeading copy="Team meetings" class="rotate-2 max-w-80" startTime="9:30" endTime="10:00" theme="teal" />

- **30min** twice a week or 60min weekly - depends on the type of the team
- **Keep it light**, prioritize chit-chat and anything that engages the whole team
- Get everyone's status so you and the team knows what's going on
- Good place to send a praise or demo some new development
- Update your team on organizational changes, events, upcoming holidays, vacations etc.

<CalendarEntryHeading
  copy="Project meetings"
  class="-rotate-1 max-w-80"
  startTime="1:30"
  endTime="3:00"
  theme="purple"
/>

- Strictly business meetings with clear goals
- Place and moment where you'll drive results
- Plan and/or solve engineering problems

<CalendarEntryHeading
  copy="Your contributions"
  class="rotate-2 max-w-80"
  startTime="3:00"
  endTime="4:00"
  theme="purple"
/>

- Schedule dedicated time for all-the-things in your calendar OR/AND use a TODO list - personally I just stick to TODO and every gap in my calendar is dedicated to it.
