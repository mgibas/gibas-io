Your job is much more complicated than this, but in general you can distill it to 3 objectives:

<ul class=" not-prose grid gap-4 my-12 sm:grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-3">
  <GridListItem theme="purple" title="Results">
    <ArrowTrendingUp slot="icon" />
    Revenue, new features, better performance, SLA - different things for everyone but end of the day it's the ultimate goal that supports and drives the business you work for.
    
    I'll use <Badge theme="purple" copy="result" /> badge to mark anything that is focused on this aspect.
  </GridListItem>

<GridListItem theme="teal" title="Team building">
  <UserGroup slot="icon" />
  Creating a cohesive, synchronized and energized group of people that can tackle any task together. Recruiting,
  bonding, resolving any issues, and creating a place where everyone wants to contribute to is the name of the game.

I'll use <Badge theme="teal" copy="team" /> badge to mark anything that is focused on this aspect.

</GridListItem>

  <GridListItem theme="yellow" title="Individual growth">
    <UserPlus slot="icon" />
    Coaching, mentoring, actively thinking about others careers, finding your replacement and letting others achieve their full potential.

    I'll use <Badge theme="yellow" copy="growth" /> badge to mark anything that is focused on this aspect.

  </GridListItem>
</ul>

That's it - just 3 things! Many books have been written for each one of them and stay assured, I'll definitely give you some quick run-down for all of them!
