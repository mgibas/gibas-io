Your job is much more complicated than this, but in general you can distill it to 3 objectives:

- **Results:**
  Revenue, new features, better performance, SLA - different things for everyone but end of the day it's the ultimate goal that supports and drives the business you work for.

- **Team building:**
  Creating a cohesive, synchronized and energized group of people that can tackle any task together. Recutting, bonding, resolving any issues, and creating a place where everyone wants to contribute to is the name of the game.
- **Individual growth:**
  Coaching, mentoring, actively thinking about others careers, finding your replacement and letting others achieve their full potential.

That's it - just 3 things! Many books have been written for each one of them and stay assured, I'll definitely give you some quick run-down for all of them!
